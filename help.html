Help

Set up, gestures and fixes.

Set up

  1. Buy WristWand from the Mac App Store and open it on your Mac. The Apple Watch app installs with it.
  2. Open WristWand on your watch and keep it open.
  3. Allow Bluetooth when asked, then wait until the Mac shows the watch as connected.
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn WristWand on.
  5. Tap your wrist twice to start. Hold your hand palm down and still, then move your wrist to move the cursor.

Permissions

AccessibilityNeeded to move the pointer, click and press keys. Without it nothing happens.
BluetoothConnects the watch to your Mac.
Microphone and Speech RecognitionOnly for voice typing.
Motion (watch)Lets the watch read its motion sensors.

Gestures

Tap wrist twiceTurn on or off.
Move wrist, palm downMove the cursor.
PinchClick. Pinch twice for double click, three times for right click.
Make a fistPress and hold to drag. Pinch to let go. Make a fist twice for right click.
Palm upScroll.
Hand on its sideZoom. Swipe to switch apps.
ShakeOpen the app switcher. In presenting, next slide.
Raise watch to mouthStart voice typing.

Turn it off fast

Press ⌃⌥⌘P or use the switch in the menu bar.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happensCheck Accessibility. After an update, remove WristWand from the list and add it again.
Cursor stops or lagsKeep the watch app open and the watch near the Mac. Make sure the watch screen stays on the app.
Pinch becomes a click you did not wantKeep your hand still while you pinch. Fast arm movement can look like a pinch.
Mac does not see the watchTurn Bluetooth off and on, then reopen the app on both devices.

Requirements

A Mac with macOS 14 or later and an Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Tested on Apple Watch Series 9 worn on the left wrist.
